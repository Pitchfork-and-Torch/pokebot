# Skill: grill-the-trainer

When: first run, or whenever the Index is empty.

Inputs: free-text answers.

Sequence:
1. What work actually pays or must not fail?
2. Which tools already exist?
3. What must never happen (contact, spend, merge, publish)?
4. How many bots can they stand seeing every day? Default 6.
5. How close are they to the 50 cap?

Validation: do not proceed to induct until risk and cap are answered.

Output: a short trainer card (work, tools, risk, cap, patience).

Approval: none. This is questions only.
